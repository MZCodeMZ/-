## 2) ATE-aligned outcomes headings (reporting frame)

### Mode shift
- Primary KPI: **car share ↓ / walk-cycle-bus share ↑** (percentage points).

### Safety perception
- Parent/pupil perceived route safety, confidence to travel independently, and school-gate conflict incidents.

### Health outcomes proxy
- Active minutes per pupil/day (or week), attendance/tardiness proxy, and self-reported wellbeing proxy.

### Carbon and air-quality co-benefits
- Estimated tCO₂e avoided from reduced car trips; school-gate idling and pollutant proxy trend.

### Inclusion/accessibility
- Participation and outcome deltas for priority cohorts (FSM, SEND/disability, EAL, low-car households, girls).

## 3) Evidence cards (intervention-level)

> **How to use:** Duplicate these cards for each school or LA and update KPI deltas with local results.

### Evidence Card: School streets
- **Target behavior:** Reduce private car drop-off at school gate; increase walk/park-and-stride.
- **Mechanism:** Timed traffic restriction at pick-up/drop-off creates safer, calmer street environment.
- **KPI delta (pilot):**
  - Car mode share: [−X pp]
  - Walk share: [+X pp]
  - Parent safety perception: [+X pp]
  - Idling incidents: [−X%]
- **Confidence level:** [High/Medium/Low] based on [sample size, consistency across schools, data quality].
- **Risks:** Traffic displacement; resident opposition; enforcement drift.
- **Mitigations:** Boundary tuning, resident engagement, ANPR/civil enforcement, alternative loading plans.

### Evidence Card: Walking bus
- **Target behavior:** Shift short-distance car trips to accompanied walking.
- **Mechanism:** Structured, supervised route with fixed stops/times reduces parent coordination burden and safety anxiety.
- **KPI delta (pilot):**
  - Walking participation: [+X pupils/week]
  - Car trips under 2 km: [−X%]
  - Attendance punctuality: [+X pp]
- **Confidence level:** [High/Medium/Low] based on [route retention, repeat participation, school consistency].
- **Risks:** Volunteer burnout; weather fragility; safeguarding workload.
- **Mitigations:** Volunteer rota depth, wet-weather protocol, safeguarding SOPs, school staff back-up cover.

### Evidence Card: Cycle training
- **Target behavior:** Increase cycling uptake and independent active travel confidence.
- **Mechanism:** Skills progression (on- and off-road) improves competence, risk perception, and parent trust.
- **KPI delta (pilot):**
  - Cycle mode share: [+X pp]
  - Post-training confidence score: [+X points]
  - Near-miss self-reports: [−X%]
- **Confidence level:** [High/Medium/Low] based on [pre/post matched surveys, completion rates].
- **Risks:** Bike access inequality; route infrastructure gaps; seasonal decline.
- **Mitigations:** Loan bikes/maintenance grants, route audits with highways, spring/autumn training blocks.

### Evidence Card: Bus incentives
- **Target behavior:** Shift medium-distance school trips from car to scheduled bus.
- **Mechanism:** Fare relief + simplified eligibility + clear journey information lowers financial and cognitive barriers.
- **KPI delta (pilot):**
  - Bus ridership to school: [+X%]
  - Car mode share (2–5 km): [−X pp]
  - Cost burden (self-reported): [−X points]
- **Confidence level:** [High/Medium/Low] based on [ticketing data coverage, persistence over terms].
- **Risks:** Service reliability; cliff-edge when subsidy ends; fraud/leakage.
- **Mitigations:** Operator performance clauses, taper design, digital validation and periodic audits.

### Evidence Card: Parent nudges
- **Target behavior:** Encourage repeat active-travel choices among “sometimes-car” households.
- **Mechanism:** Timely prompts, social norm messaging, commitment devices, and personalized route prompts.
- **KPI delta (pilot):**
  - Active-trip frequency: [+X trips/household/week]
  - Campaign engagement rate: [X%]
  - Car-use relapse after holidays: [−X pp]
- **Confidence level:** [High/Medium/Low] based on [A/B test quality, engagement-to-behavior linkage].
- **Risks:** Message fatigue; digital exclusion; low salience.
- **Mitigations:** Cadence caps, offline channels, segmented messaging, term-time trigger calendar.
