# KIGO Pre-Seed Fundraising Deck

## How to use this file
- **Copy/paste into slides**: Treat each numbered section as a slide. The bullets under each title are slide copy.
- **Design cues**: Follow the “Slide layout & design” notes under each slide for visuals, hierarchy, and emphasis.
- **Color palette (suggested)**: Deep navy (#0A1F44), mint (#31D0AA), warm yellow (#F5C451), soft gray (#F4F6F8), safety red (#E74C3C for alerts/safeguarding badges).
- **Fonts**: Headings—Inter Bold (or similar); Body—Inter Regular; Numbers/callouts—Inter Medium.
- **Iconography**: Use simple line icons for safety, routing/paths, shield/compliance, family, analytics.

## 1) Vision & Mission
- **Vision:** Safer, greener, lower-stress daily school travel for every child, family, and community.
- **Mission:** Provide an AI-enabled platform that orchestrates safer school journeys, shared travel, and verified safeguarding—built for parents, schools, and local authorities.

**Slide layout & design**
- Full-bleed hero image of children/parents at a school gate with a semi-transparent navy overlay.
- Left column: headline + 2 bullets; right column: a single bold stat or promise (e.g., “Zero unsafe handoffs”).
- Add a shield icon + small “Safeguarding-first” tag for immediate positioning.

## 2) Problem
- Parents face safety anxiety, congestion, and time loss on the school run; councils face rising costs and environmental pressure.
- Schools lack a unified tool to manage safeguarding, attendance on transport, and communication with families.
- Existing solutions are fragmented (routing, payments, safeguarding, communications) and fail to deliver proof of compliance or measurable impact.

**Slide layout & design**
- Three pain columns: Parents / Schools / Councils. Use icons (heart shield, clipboard, city hall) and 1–2 bullets each.
- Add a red “Risk” pill and a gray “Fragmented” pill near the subhead to reinforce urgency.

## 3) Solution (KIGO Platform)
- **Parent-first UX** with real-time safeguarding visibility, consent, and notifications.
- **AI-enabled routing & shared-journey matching** to reduce car trips and improve capacity use across walking buses, shuttles, and carpools.
- **Safeguarding & compliance by design:** digital registers, incident workflows, risk flags, and auditable reports for schools/LAs.
- **Data layer** that connects parents, schools, and councils—unlocking funding and policy alignment.

**Slide layout & design**
- Split screen: left side “How it works” (3 steps); right side phone mock (parent view) + laptop mock (school/LA console).
- Use mint highlight for “real-time” elements and navy for compliance.

## 4) Product Snapshot (Early MVP)
- Multi-modal support: walking bus, minibus/coach, carpool, public transit handoffs.
- Parent app: opt-in travel plans, guardian handoffs, live ETA and attendance confirmation.
- School/LA console: route design, capacity planning, real-time safeguarding alerts, compliance reporting.
- Integrations: student information systems, payments/consents, ID validation, and analytics exports.

**Slide layout & design**
- Four-tile grid: “Modes”, “Parent app”, “School/LA console”, “Integrations”. Each tile gets an icon and a one-liner.
- Optional mini-flow ribbon at the bottom showing Morning → In-transit → Arrival → Audit trail.

## 5) Market Opportunity
- **UK school journeys:** 2.78M daily car trips; £458M+ annual parent spend; £2.3B+ LA school transport budgets.
- **Global tailwind:** congestion, emissions mandates, and safeguarding requirements make school mobility a priority.
- **Initial beachhead:** councils under SEND/ESG pressure + academy trusts seeking safer, lower-cost mobility.

**Slide layout & design**
- Use a 3-number hero row with big numerals; add short captions below (Car Trips / Parent Spend / LA Budgets).
- Map visual (UK focus) with arrows for “Beachhead → Expansion”.

## 6) Traction & Evidence
- MVP completed safeguarding-compliant core: digital registers, reporting, parent/school/LA dashboards.
- Early pilots (Chelmsford & Epping) to generate evidence for council procurement.
- Targeting **20K users across 10 schools by end of Year 1** to validate activation/retention and LA procurement metrics.

**Slide layout & design**
- Timeline or milestone dots (MVP → Pilots → 20K users). Use mint for achieved, yellow for in-progress.
- Include 1–2 testimonial placeholders with quotation marks for pilot feedback.

## 7) Business Model
- **Licences:** school subscriptions; local authority licences (per pupil / per cluster); academy trust packages.
- **Premium features:** safeguarding analytics, behavioural programmes, compliance add-ons, sustainability reporting.
- **Transaction/usage:** parent perks, sponsored campaigns, verified advertising, and aggregated insights (opt-in, anonymised).

**Slide layout & design**
- Three pricing pillars with headers (School, LA, Add-ons). Add “value metric” tags (per pupil, per route, per cluster).
- Small flywheel graphic showing data → insights → monetisation (anonymised) → better routing.

## 8) Go-To-Market (Council-first, AI-enabled)
- **Targeting/ICP:** SEND-heavy councils, community action schools, PTAs, academy trusts; focus on safety/compliance pain.
- **Sales engine:** proof-of-value pilots → safety/attendance evidence → scale via MAT/LA frameworks; AI-driven route optimisation to compress payback.
- **Channels:** direct to schools/academies; councils & LAs; parent communities; partners (transport ops, insurance, ESG sponsors).
- **Activation:** rapid onboarding flows; templates for routes and safeguarding plans; measurable KPIs (attendance accuracy, reduced car trips, incident response time).

**Slide layout & design**
- Funnel visual: Awareness → Pilot → Evidence pack → Framework win → Rollout. Call out KPIs under each step.
- Add a “30–60–90 day pilot” stripe with deliverables and owners.

## 9) Competitive Advantage
- Only platform combining **parent-grade UX**, **shared-journey orchestration**, **safeguarding-grade compliance**, and **LA-scale routing/BI** in one solution.
- Faster evaluation: behavioural science-based change programmes + COM-B evaluation tools baked in.
- Defensibility via data network effects (usage improves routing/safety models), integrations, and policy alignment with councils.

**Slide layout & design**
- 2x2 matrix: “UX quality” vs “Compliance depth”; plot KIGO in top-right; competitors clustered elsewhere.
- Badge row for moats: Data network, Compliance by design, Behavioural playbooks, Integrations.

## 10) IP & Data Protection Roadmap
- **Pre-funding:** secure code, design docs, data schemas; open-source components with proprietary safeguards; NDAs with partners.
- **Pilot year:** harden intelligence engine, safeguarding flows, COM-B playbooks; register trademarks and copyright.
- **Months 0–12 post-funding:** file UK/EU design rights, provisional patents for AI routing + safeguarding pipelines; SOC2-ready controls.
- **Year 2+:** extend marks internationally; behavioural and demand-side datasets; bias-monitoring and explainability for LA compliance.

**Slide layout & design**
- Roadmap horizontal lane with time buckets (Pre-funding, Pilot year, 0–12m, 12–24m+). Use lock/shield icons for security.
- Add a compliance checklist sidebar (GDPR, safeguarding standards, SOC2-ready).

## 11) Financial Outlook (5-Year Path)
- Break-even in **Year 3** driven by LA programmes and school licences; projected **70–75% gross margin by Year 4**.
- Sustainable ARR **~£1.8M in Year 3** at 50–60 schools + 2–3 LAs; expansion upside via parent and sponsor revenues.
- Key metrics: CAC payback <12 months; activation/retention cohorts; LTV/CAC >3x; unit-level gross margin improvements through automation.

**Slide layout & design**
- Simple line chart for ARR + gross margin over 5 years. Use navy line for ARR, mint for margin %.
- Add a compact KPI box: CAC payback, LTV/CAC, gross margin.

## 12) Funding Ask (£750K SEIS/EIS)
- **Runway:** 18–24 months to secure first LA contract and scale pilots.
- **Use of funds:** ~45% technology development (core platform, AI routing, safeguarding, LA dashboards); ~17% pilots & behavioural programmes; ~17% community engagement; ~21% ops/compliance.
- **Milestones:** safeguarding-complete MVP; 20K users / 10 schools; first LA contract; validated evidence pack for procurement.

**Slide layout & design**
- Pie or stacked bar for use-of-funds. Overlay each slice with milestone icons.
- Add “Funding round structure” chip (SEIS/EIS) and a clear CTA button-style shape: “Join the round”.

## 13) Team & Advisors
- Multidisciplinary team across transport tech, behavioural science, product design, and public-sector evaluation.
- Experience spanning EV manufacturing, large-scale routing, UX for families, and council procurement.
- Advisors include specialists in child safety, compliance, governance, and public-sector innovation.

**Slide layout & design**
- Photo grid with names, roles, 1-line credibility. Use navy cards with mint underline.
- Add advisor strip with logos/affiliations where allowed.

## 14) Why Now / Call to Action
- Councils and parents urgently seek safer, greener school mobility; regulatory and ESG pressures are increasing budgets for compliant solutions.
- KIGO offers a unified, compliance-ready platform with measurable impact on safety, congestion, and emissions.
- **Ask:** £750K to complete safeguarding-grade MVP, validate LA pilots, and build the go-to-market engine for council-led scale.

**Slide layout & design**
- Bold headline + subhead. Right side: checklist of immediate next steps (e.g., “Schedule pilot review”, “Meet the product team”).
- Reinforce urgency with a short “Why now” stat bar.
